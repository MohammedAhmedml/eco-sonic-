## Selection Notes

Not every listed item must be installed simultaneously. Items such as Arduino boards are retained as auxiliary/alternative development hardware while the ESP32 remains the primary controller. RO, UV-C, and membrane stages are experimental and require appropriate safety and engineering validation.
