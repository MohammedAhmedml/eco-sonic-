# Safety and Limitations

## Electrical Safety

- Use appropriate fuses.
- Keep wiring away from uncontrolled water exposure.
- Use insulated terminals.
- Use a main switch and emergency cutoff.
- Verify sensor input ranges before connection to the ESP32.

## UV-C Safety

UV-C must be contained in a properly designed enclosure. Do not operate an exposed UV-C source around people.

## Water Safety

Experimental water is not potable. Do not drink prototype water.

## Mechanical Safety

Protect rotating water-wheel and generator components and secure shafts, couplings, and bearings.

## Engineering Limitations

The prototype cannot reproduce all hydraulic, thermal, chemical, biological, and operational conditions of a commercial data center.
