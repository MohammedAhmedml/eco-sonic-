# Innovation

Eco-Sonic combines three experimental themes in one prototype:

- Water resource recovery.
- Low-grade waste-energy harvesting.
- Embedded sensing and control.

The innovation is evaluated at system level rather than by claiming that any individual component is novel.

## Research Questions

1. How much water can be recovered under defined test conditions?
2. What electrical output can be obtained from a controlled hydraulic flow?
3. What output can be obtained from controlled vibration?
4. How does operating condition affect the harvested output?
5. Can the ESP32 provide useful low-cost monitoring and control?

## Evidence Standard

Claims about performance will only be added after measurement and documented testing.
