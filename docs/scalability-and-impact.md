# Scalability and Impact

## Potential Scaling Areas

- Larger water-treatment modules.
- Industrial water-quality instrumentation.
- Improved hydraulic turbines.
- Dedicated vibration-harvesting mounts.
- Networked monitoring.
- Integration with facility-management systems.

## Important Scaling Considerations

Full-scale implementation would require engineering validation of:

- Water chemistry.
- Microbiological safety.
- Pressure and flow requirements.
- Pumping energy.
- Materials compatibility.
- Maintenance.
- Electrical safety.
- Regulatory requirements.
- Total lifecycle cost.

The prototype provides a research platform rather than a direct industrial deployment design.
