# Working Principle

## 1. Water Loop

The pump moves water through the experimental treatment and hydraulic harvesting loop.

## 2. Water Treatment

Selected filtration stages are used according to the experimental objective and feedwater characteristics.

## 3. Hydraulic Harvesting

The water wheel converts hydraulic motion into mechanical rotation. A small generator converts mechanical rotation into electrical output.

## 4. Vibration Harvesting

Piezoelectric elements convert mechanical deformation into an electrical signal.

## 5. Monitoring

The ESP32 reads sensors and records operating conditions.

## 6. Evaluation

Measured voltage, current, flow, temperature, and water-quality values are analyzed to determine prototype performance.
